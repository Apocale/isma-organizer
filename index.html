Scope

It only files what is loose

Organize files what sits at the root of the project and on top of its numbered bins. A bin you made yourself keeps exactly what you put in it.

Sequences

Nests apart from edits

A sequence used inside another timeline is a nest. Words you choose can force either: "Hook" always an edit, "Cut" always nested.

Undo

One click puts it back

Undo returns each item to the bin it came from, with its old label color, and removes the bins that run created.

Search Pinterest from the panel

Type what you need, press Enter: Pinterest's videos show up as tiles. Click one to save it.

2  Keep

All your saves in one place

Every video you saved, newest first, right next to the timeline. Type a few words to filter, click to watch it large with sound.

3  Drop

Drag it onto the timeline

Drag a tile onto the timeline or into a bin. Clips already in your project carry a badge, so you never import twice.

Videos are saved in the panel's own folder, out of your Downloads; the Folder button opens it. Most Pinterest videos need ffmpeg to join picture and sound: brew install ffmpeg on a Mac, winget install -e --id Gyan.FFmpeg on Windows.

06  FAQ

Questions editors ask.

Does it touch the bins I made?

No. It files what is new: the root of the project and the top of its own numbered bins. Your bins keep what you put in them. One option, off for new users, Merge old and imported bins, also empties the plugin's bins that an imported project left inside yours.

Can I undo a run?

Yes, every one. Undo last organize puts each item back where it was, with its previous label color, and removes the bins that run created.

How does it tell nests from edits?

By reading your timelines: a sequence used inside another one is a nest and goes to 08. Premiere's own "Nested Sequence 12" always is. Every other sequence is an edit and goes to 01. Words you choose in the settings can force either way.

Is it fast on big projects?

The automatic passes, after a Nest or an import, read only what changed. A click on Organize reads every timeline once: about 5 seconds on a real project with 14 000 clips in its timelines.

Which Premiere versions? Windows?

Premiere Pro 2022 to 2026, on Mac and Windows. It is made and tested on Premiere Pro 2026 on a Mac; Windows and older versions are supported by design but not yet tested on a real machine. If something breaks, open an issue on GitHub.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me?

The installer is not signed. On a Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. On Windows: More info › Run anyway.

Does it send anything online?

No account, no tracking. Only the Pinterest searches and downloads you ask for go to Pinterest. Settings, logs and thumbnails stay on your computer.

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT license.
